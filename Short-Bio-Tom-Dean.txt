Tom Dean is a Customer Success Engineer at Solo.io, where he works with customers using its cloud connectivity and agentic AI products. He started programming in BASIC on Apple IIs at the age of ten and learned UNIX as an undergraduate at Purdue University. Since then he has spent three decades in infrastructure, from Linux in the data center to Kubernetes and service mesh. Before Solo.io he was a field engineer at Buoyant, the company behind Linkerd, and he co-presented a talk at KubeCon North America 2024. He has written many courses for Linux Academy and A Cloud Guru and hosted Linux This Month on several occasions. In his spare time, he enjoys motorcycling, music, and spending time with his son, Tucker.
